Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Ruler handles information when you use the app.

We respect your privacy and have designed the app so that measurements and other information generated during normal use can remain stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address in order to use the app.

We do not collect or store personal information through the app.

Measurement information entered or generated while using the app is not uploaded to our servers.

3. Local Storage

Information that the app needs to save for its functionality may be stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Because locally stored information remains on your device, we do not have access to it and cannot retrieve it for you if it is deleted or lost.

4. Advertising and Analytics

The app does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising services.

The app does not use third-party analytics services to track your activity or usage for analytics purposes.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because information generated during normal use of the app is not uploaded to our servers, we do not have access to such information.

6. Device Permissions

The app may request access to certain device features only when such access is necessary to provide a specific function of the app.

Any information accessed through such device features is used only for the corresponding function and is not collected, sold, or shared with third parties unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy.

7. Data Deletion

You may delete information stored by the app using the available features within the app, where applicable.

You may also remove the app and its locally stored data from your device. Deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored information.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
